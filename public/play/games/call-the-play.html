For grown-ups: a decision tree under constraints — the same reasoning shape as an if / else if / else in code, and the concept behind Higher or Lower and In the Club. Every call is graded best, reasonable or poor rather than right or wrong, because real football usually has more than one defensible option; the reasoning is always explained afterwards. Teaches down and distance, field position, kicking range and clock management. Ages 7–10.
