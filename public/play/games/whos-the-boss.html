For grown-ups: real-world civics through real, current heads of state and government — 200+ leaders across every continent, sourced from the site's own leaders dataset. Level 1 (ages 5–7) is the classic US/UK three-way split. Level 2, "Who Leads?" (ages 7–10), pairs a leader's name with their country, with same-continent distractors so a correct guess takes geography, not luck. Level 3, "What's the Job?" (ages 8–11), is the real lesson: President, Prime Minister, King or Queen, Chancellor and Supreme Leader are different jobs, and dozens of countries — the UK included — split head of state and head of government between two different people. Distractors and question order are Leitner-weighted per PLAY-MASTERY-SPEC.md §1.7, so a missed leader resurfaces sooner than one already known cold.
