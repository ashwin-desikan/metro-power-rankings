## Editorial discipline

Every peg taken should funnel back to a canonical destination on rankings.citizenofnowhere.org (a metro page, a badge page, or a matchup page). The peg is the entry point, not the conclusion. If a peg cannot be tied to at least one badge or methodology anchor without forcing it, the piece probably belongs on a different Substack.

Items should age out of this watchlist when the news cycle resolves them; archive them under a "Pegs taken" or "Pegs missed" log with a one-line note on what was published or why nothing was. Monthly review cycle is sufficient; do not re-litigate the watchlist weekly.
