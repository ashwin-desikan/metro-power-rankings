Round 7: venue dedup, transit stations per entry, collapsible subgroups

- Transit: Metro System and Suburban Rail now carry per-entry station counts
  through the ETL (Culture-Infra column P). The frontend shows each line's
  station count on its own card ("London Underground / 272 stations",
  "Elizabeth Line / 23 stations", etc.) while the dimension aggregate is
  preserved in the section heading.

- Notable Venues, Stage 1 (ETL): manual canonicalization of venue names for
  the two cases where the same physical venue appears under slightly
  different names across sports. "New Wembley Stadium" (Football) and
  "Wembley Stadium" (Combat Sports) now both normalize to "Wembley
  Stadium"; "The O2 Arena" (Tennis) and "O2 Arena" (Combat Sports) both
  normalize to "O2 Arena". Applied in _normalize_venue_name, scoped to
  rows whose league is Notable Venues so team names aren't touched.

- Notable Venues, Stage 2 (render): dedupe by name, aggregate all sports
  into a single card ("Stade de France / Olympics/Athletics, Football,
  Rugby Union" renders once instead of three times). Ordering priority:
  Olympics/Athletics first, then Football, then American Football, then
  the marquee team-sport family (Basketball, Baseball, Test/T20 Cricket,
  Rugby Union/League, Hockey), then everything else. A venue's sports
  are sorted internally by the same priority, so the card's lead sport
  drives its position in the outer list.

- Museum/Landmark: the four buckets (Museums & Galleries, Performing
  Arts Venues, Parks/Zoos/Theme Parks, Landmarks & Civic) are now
  collapsible cards with item counts, collapsed by default. Matches the
  Major Sporting Events and Other Teams UX.

- Infrastructure: each type (Airport, Port, Metro System, Suburban Rail,
  Notable Train Hubs, Trade Venue, Central Bank, Stock Exchange, Data
  Center Hub, Internet Exchange, Military Base, Notable Bridges) is
  now a collapsible card. Same pattern.

- Luxury Hospitality: each category (Michelin 3 Star, 2 Star, 1 Star,
  Luxury Hotel, etc.) collapses by default.

- Data source cleanup: extract_teams no longer reads the RegTeams sheet.
  Team List is now the single source of truth for teams. RegTeams held
  128 rows across 115 metros, almost all tagged with stale seasons
  (97 rows from 2023, 25 from 2024), including London Irish which was
  suspended from Premiership Rugby in 2023 but still rendered on the
  London page. Dropping RegTeams removes London Irish and any other
  stale legacy entries.

- Information architecture: new "Education & Research" H2 section on the
  metro detail page, sitting between Cultural Assets and Infrastructure.
  Top 2000 Universities, Top 250 Hospitals, and Top Research Institutions
  now live under this H2 instead of under Cultural Assets. Each of the
  three renders as a collapsible card (collapsed by default, item count
  in the header), matching the Infrastructure / Luxury / Other Teams UX.
  The existing #universities anchor is preserved on the Universities
  details element so deep links keep working.

- Label: the metro overview "State/Province:" field is renamed
  "Primary State/Province:" to make it clear we're showing the metro's
  primary administrative division, not a full list.

- xlsx refresh: MetroAreas.xlsx updated to the 42ff1615 upload. All
  downstream JSON regenerated.
