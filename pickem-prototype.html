How scoring works

The Slate — 10 pts per correct call. Premier League games are three-way (home / draw / away); NFL games are two-way. Your running streak counts consecutive correct calls across both leagues in kickoff order.

Confidence — rank your slate; slot value = points if that pick lands (10 games: top slot worth 10, bottom worth 1). A perfect PL matchweek is 55 bonus points.

Upset Radar — the five games where model and market disagree most. Side with one source for +25 if it grades closer to the result. Scored by Brier, the same metric the prediction hubs already publish.

Beat the Model — the model’s own card is graded with identical rules. Finish a week with more slate points than the machine and it goes on your permanent record.
