Round 6: xlsx refresh, College/University teams bucket, Museum buckets, collapsible subgroups

- Data refresh: MetroAreas.xlsx updated with bug fix from the prior version.
  Full ETL re-run: 4,283 metros, all detail JSON, metros.json, regions.json,
  and meta.json regenerated.

- ETL: America's Cup override in extract.py. All ten America's Cup rows in
  Culture-Infra are tagged Annual=Y in the xlsx (Auckland, Barcelona, San
  Diego, Newport, Isle of Wight, Valencia, Naples, Fremantle, San Francisco,
  New York Harbor) but semantically each edition is a championship moment,
  not a recurring annual event. ETL now strips the annual flag for rows
  whose name starts with "America's Cup" and type is "Sporting Event",
  routing them into Championship Finals alongside Super Bowl / World Series.

- Other Teams: new College/University Teams subgroup covering both men's
  and women's collegiate sport (FBS, FCS, NCAA, NCAA W, College Hockey).
  1,056 team rows across 365 metros. Takes precedence over the football
  and gender partitions so a W Basketball NCAA W team lands in college,
  not in Other Women's Teams.

- Other Teams and Notable Venues are now collapsible cards matching the
  Major Sporting Events UX: each subgroup shows label + item count and
  expands on click. Four collapsible subgroups under Other Teams
  (Football/Soccer, College/University, Other Men's, Other Women's) and
  one under Major League Teams/Venues (Notable Venues). Major League
  Teams remains a flat, always-expanded grid because it's the marquee
  roster and worth the real estate.

- Museum/Landmark restructure: 1,147 items across 20 subtypes now
  bucketed into four smart categories with even distribution:
    Museums & Galleries (Art, History, Science, Cultural Heritage,
      Archaeology; ~504 rows)
    Performing Arts Venues (Concert Hall, Opera House, Theatre,
      Amphitheatre; ~203 rows)
    Parks, Zoos & Theme Parks (Park/Garden, Zoo, Aquarium, Theme Park;
      ~244 rows)
    Landmarks & Civic (Memorial, Religious site, Government,
      International Organization, Library, Shopping, Film Hub;
      ~196 rows; also catches any unknown/new subtypes)
